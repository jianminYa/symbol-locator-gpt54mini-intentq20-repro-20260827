# Privacy and artifact exclusions

The staging tree was scanned with the available supplementary patterns for
OpenAI/GitHub/AWS keys, bearer and authorization headers, cookies, private
keys, and credential-bearing URL queries. The final scan found zero hits. No
`.env` or `.secrets` file was copied.

The publishing environment did not have `gitleaks` installed, so the
repository-wide supplementary scanner was used and its zero-hit result is
recorded below and in the final publication verification.
The supplementary rules distinguish source-code object-key labels such as
`Authorization: <expression>` from a serialized credential-bearing header.
